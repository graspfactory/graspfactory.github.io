We introduce GraspFactory, a dataset containing over 109 million two-fingered 6-DoF grasps collectively for the Franka Panda (with 14,690 objects) and Robotiq 2F85 grippers (with 33,710 objects). GraspFactory is designed for training data-intensive models, and we demonstrate the generalization capabilities of a model trained on the Franka Panda subset of GraspFactory in both simulated and real-world settings.
